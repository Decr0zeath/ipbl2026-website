Note · Kit contents

Each delegate receives one (1) printed PCB, twelve (12) SG90 servos, one (1) Arduino Nano (ATmega328P, USB-C), and a small bag of the complete quadruped parts and components that needs to be soldered. The remaining items in the bill of materials below, including batteries, capacitors, buck converter, and consumables, are the responsibility of each team.

Important · The buck converter is not optional

The 2S pack delivers 7.4 V nominal. The SG90 servos are rated for 4.8–6 V, and the Nano's logic rail requires a clean 5 V; neither can be supplied directly from the battery. The XL4015E1 (U2) steps 7.4 V down to a regulated 5 V before either load receives it. Omitting this step and wiring the battery directly to the servo headers or the Nano's 5 V pin will overdrive the servos past their rated voltage and pass more current through the Nano's onboard regulator than it is designed to handle. Set and verify the converter's output before it is installed on the board; see Section 04 for the full two-rail rationale and Section 05 for the calibration procedure.

Optional but Recommended

  • Bench power supply: current-limited, for first-power-on tests before committing the battery. See the buck converter verification step in Section 05.
  • Helping hands / PCB holder: markedly improves soldering quality and consistency.
  • Logic analyser: not essential, but useful for debugging servo PWM signals if problems arise.
  • Magnifying lamp: substantially eases work with small components and fine solder joints.
