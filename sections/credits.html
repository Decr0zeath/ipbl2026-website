Original Platform

The quadruped described in this manual is based on the open-hardware "Quadruped Spider Robot" published by Regis Hsu on PCBWay. His original design, the chassis geometry, the eight-servo layout, and the SG90/Nano combination, forms the foundation extended for iPBL 2026.

Source · Regis Hsu

The base mechanical platform and original concept originate from Regis Hsu's project on PCBWay: Quadruped Spider Robot: 3D Printed Parts, SG90 Servos, Arduino Nano. This adaptation modifies the PCB for the programme's pedagogical needs and adds calibration, gait, and testing material; the underlying mechanical design and spirit of the original project remain his.

About the International Project-Based Learning Programme

iPBL is a collaborative initiative between partner universities in the Asia-Pacific region, rotating each year between member institutions. The programme brings undergraduate engineering and computer science students together for an intensive in-person collaborative build, with a project that is partially prepared in advance and substantially extended on-site.

The 2026 edition, hosted in Cebu by USJ-R, chose the quadruped robotic platform for its balance of accessibility (every delegate can complete the pre-training individually) and depth (the on-ground project layers in elements that none of them could have anticipated from the manual alone).

The Manual Itself

This pre-training manual was prepared by the USJ-R host team in early 2026, drawing on:

  • The mechanical platform of Regis Hsu, as published on PCBWay
  • The Arduino Servo library documentation and reference implementations
  • Troubleshooting notes gathered from two pilot builds conducted with USJ-R students in late 2025
  • Feedback from prior iPBL editions on pre-training scope and pacing
