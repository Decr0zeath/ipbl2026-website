A field manual for
assembling a quadruped
robot, screw to gait.

Welcome, and maayong adlaw! This is the field manual for iPBL 2026, the International Project-Based Learning programme: everything needed to build the machine the rest of the programme is built upon, a four-legged, twelve-servo robot driven by an Arduino Nano and a custom PCB.

This manual walks you through every screw, every solder joint, every line of firmware, from a box of parts to a robot that stands and crawls. That is only half of what iPBL asks of you; giving the robot a brain and a pair of eyes comes next, in Cebu.

Deliberately narrow, by design.

This manual is deliberately narrow in scope. Its purpose is a working understanding of the machine underneath the programme, nothing more. Assemble the body, solder the PCB, mount and calibrate the servos, set the rocker arms, and bring the unit to a working crawl: that is the entire brief, from parts to a robot that stands, blinks, and awaits instructions. Giving that robot a brain and a pair of eyes, a Raspberry Pi, a NoIR camera, and a proximity sensor, along with the collaborative brief itself, belongs to the in-person programme. It remains withheld here by design.

Covered in This Manual

  • Mechanical assembly of the chassis and twelve-servo leg system
  • STL files for every structural part, with a labelled inventory
  • PCB schematic walkthrough and hand-soldered assembly
  • Servo wiring, power distribution, and pinout reference
  • Arduino firmware: upload, calibration, and basic gait
  • A bench check against reference footage, and the handoff to Cebu

Reserved for the iPBL Programme

  • Setting up the Raspberry Pi as the robot's brain
  • Computer vision through its NoIR camera
  • Proximity sensing and obstacle awareness
  • The collaborative challenge brief (revealed Day 01)
  • Inter-team protocol and integration tasks

Eight sections, one robot that stands and walks, ready for what Cebu adds. Begin at the beginning, and do not skip ahead; every section assumes the one before it.

About this manual

The Quadruped Manual is prepared for delegates of iPBL 2026 by the host team at the University of San Jose – Recoletos. The mechanical platform is based on the open quadruped published by Mert Kilic on PCBWay, adapted and extended for our programme.
