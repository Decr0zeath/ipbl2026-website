      About this manual

      The Quadruped Pre-Training Manual is prepared for delegates of iPBL 2026 by the host team at the University of San Jose – Recoletos. The mechanical platform is based on the open quadruped published by Regis Hsu on PCBWay, adapted and extended for our programme.
