Every leg built in this section is identical in construction. Build the first carefully and deliberately; the remaining three follow by repetition. By the fourth leg, the process should feel routine; that is the intended outcome.

Warning · Centre your servos first

Before installing any servo rocker arm into a printed part, the servo positions must be moved to its mechanical centre (90°). Failing to do so results in the horn being mounted at an arbitrary angle, which is typically discovered only after assembly, when a leg is found to travel from 30° to 130° rather than the intended 0° to 180°. Centre every servo before proceeding with any screws.

Note · Photos are illustrative

The photographs in this section were not taken during a live build; the parts are shown held together for clarity, with no screws driven in. Read them as a guide to part orientation and fit, not as a record of a fully fastened assembly.

Assembly Sequence

  1. Seat a servo into each tibia

    Take a pre-centred SG90 and slide it into the tibia's servo pocket until the flange sits flush against the print. Secure with screws through the mounting tabs.

  2. Seat a servo into each coxa bracket

    Coxa brackets are handed: coxa_l for the left side, coxa_r for the right. Slide a pre-centred SG90 into the coxa pocket until the flange sits flush, then secure with screws through the mounting tabs.

  3. Secure both servos with s_hold

    Fit s_hold over the servos now mounted in the tibia and coxa, locking them in place, and drive the screws through s_hold into each servo's mounting tabs.

  4. Assemble the tibia and coxa to the femur

    Join each side's sub-assemblies through the femur, keeping the pairing handed: on the left side, tibia_l → femur → coxa_l; on the right side, tibia_r → femur → coxa_r.

  5. Mount four servos into body_u

    Seat a pre-centred SG90 into each of the four servo pockets in body_u, the upper chassis plate, and secure each with screws.

  6. Install the four limbs onto body_u

    Mount each of the four completed limbs onto its corresponding servo output shaft on body_u, from Step 5.
