What iPBL Is

iPBL brings undergraduate delegates together for a project that is partially prepared in advance and substantially extended on-site: each delegate arrives having already completed an individual pre-training build, then spends the on-ground programme extending it in ways the manual alone doesn't anticipate.

For 2026, the host team chose a quadruped robotic platform for its balance of accessibility, every delegate can complete the pre-training individually, and depth: what happens once every delegation is in the same room is reserved for the programme itself.

Four Institutions, One Cohort

iPBL 2026 draws delegates from four universities across the Philippines, Taiwan, and Japan.

University of San Jose – Recoletos

Cebu City, Philippines. Host of iPBL 2026, and prepared the pre-training manual, PCB design, kit shipments, and on-ground coordination.

Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology

Tainan, Taiwan. Sending delegates from Engineering and Computer Science programmes.

Osaka Institute of Technology

Osaka, Japan. Sending delegates from Robotics, Information Science, and Electrical Engineering.

Saint Michael's College of Laguna

Biñan, Laguna, Philippines. Sending delegates from its Computer Science programme.

The Shape of the Programme

iPBL runs in two phases: individual pre-training, completed at each delegate's home campus before travel, and an intensive in-person build once every delegation has arrived. What happens on-site builds directly on the pre-training; specifics of that phase are intentionally not published in advance.
